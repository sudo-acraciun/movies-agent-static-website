I Like Movies: press factsheet
https://ilikemovies.app/press

What it is
A free movie and TV tracker that helps you decide what to watch. It keeps a
record of every film and series you have seen, with ratings, notes and episode
progress, then uses that record to help you pick the next one: monthly
collections built from your own ratings, an assistant you can ask in plain
language, and streaming availability resolved for your country. People who
live together can share one library; friends can follow what each other is
watching. Letterboxd and IMDb exports import directly.

Platforms
iPhone on the App Store (app id 6805131346).
Android on Google Play (package com.moviesagent.app).

Price
Free. No advertising, no sponsored placements.

Launched
12 August 2026 on Google Play. September 2026 on the App Store.

Made by
Alexandru Crăciun, one developer, Netherlands.
Full-stack developer, over ten years in. I Like Movies is a solo project,
built because picking something to watch with someone else is harder than
picking alone.
